The approach

A sharp website is good. A working system is better.

Presence

Your website explains what you do, who it helps, why it matters, and how to start.

Automation

Your agents handle repeatable work, draft outputs, surface risks, and wait when approval matters.

Protection

Your tools, logins, data flows, and permissions are reviewed before automation gets deeper access.

Control

You get scope, logs, review points, fallback steps, and a cleaner way to run the operation.

Operating rule

A prompt is not a process.

The risky version of AI is giving a model access and hoping it behaves. Cyber Ethos builds controlled systems: defined jobs, limited permissions, human approval, visible logs, and security-aware handoffs.

  • Clear business objective
  • Defined tools and permissions
  • Human approval for risky actions
  • Action logs and review trails
  • Security basics before scale
  • Fallback steps when systems fail
