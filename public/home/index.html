01Transmission

In his words

I grew up dreaming about being in America, and I made it happen.

Azad Sleigher, to Welcome.US

Azad Sleigher is an Iraqi American, a former Special Operations interpreter and a U.S. Marine Corps veteran. He taught himself English by talking with American soldiers. Now he is a cybersecurity engineer, helping people secure their systems and keep them safe from hackers.

1996
Born in Baghdad. Raised in Kirkuk from age four.
2019
Landed in North Carolina on a Special Immigrant Visa.
90Days
Roughly how long the green card took after he arrived.
2022
Took the Oath of Allegiance as a U.S. citizen.

05The new front line

A life of service. A new front line.

As an interpreter, his job was to read the ground and the people on it, then give the team an honest picture, including the parts nobody wanted to hear. The job hasn’t changed. The ground has.

Cyber Ethos is his independent security practice. Written scope first. Authorization before any testing. Every finding validated, then ranked by real risk.

  1. 01

    Cybersecurity audit

    Accounts, devices, access rules and habits, reviewed and returned as written findings ranked by risk.

    Request a cybersecurity audit
  2. 02

    Penetration testing

    Authorized, scoped attack simulations with documented, reproducible paths and clear guidance to fix them.

    Request penetration testing
  3. 03

    Website vulnerability detection

    Forms, logins, payments, plugins and configuration, tested and handed back as a prioritized fix list.

    Request website vulnerability detection
